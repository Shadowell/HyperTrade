# Market Intelligence Curated Context

- funding/open-interest context should be treated as positioning and risk evidence, not as a standalone buy/sell signal.
- Rising open interest with positive funding can indicate crowded long exposure; confirm with price action, liquidity, and risk limits.
- Falling open interest during price moves can indicate position reduction rather than fresh directional conviction.
- Curated context is deterministic fallback material for offline tests and must not replace source-backed exchange metrics.
