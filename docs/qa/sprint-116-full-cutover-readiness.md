### Professional-agent comparison

The operator contract now follows the useful execution pattern shared by Codex, Claude Code and
OpenCode: task lifecycle is separate from bounded public answer events, tool/evidence work is auditable
but absent from the final answer, and an explicit final projection gives decision, evidence, unknowns
and safe next action. In trading-specific terms, direct mainnet execution is blocked before planning,
Testnet approval and excessive leverage are held for review, stale data becomes a data gap, and
in-sample/OOS conflicts preserve evidence but cannot promote a strategy automatically.

This is not a like-for-like quality ranking against those products: their proprietary tools, models
and task sets are different. HyperTrade is production-verified for its supported single-turn,
governed-research scope, not a claim of fully general-agent parity. Durable multi-turn
conversation/context resolution remains a separately scoped product capability.
